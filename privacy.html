YOUR INFORMATION

Fintale privacy notice

Updated 28 October 2026

You can play the demo without sharing your email or feedback. Signing up for launch updates is optional.

Who runs Fintale?

Fintale is an independent project by Marko Simovic. For questions about your information, or to request access, correction or deletion, email simovicm26@gmail.com.

Lesson progress on your device

The demo stores your current lesson step, completed activities and XP in your browser’s local storage so you can return to your place. This lesson progress is not sent to Fintale. Use Restart lesson or clear this website’s browser data to remove it.

Feedback and launch updates

If you submit the signup form, Brevo receives your email, your launch-update consent and any optional answers you included: what you liked, what could improve, and which finance, economics and accounting topics you want next. Feedback helps guide development. Your email is used to confirm signup and send Fintale launch updates.

You choose whether to subscribe by checking the launch-update box. You can withdraw that consent using the unsubscribe link in our emails. If you skip signup, your feedback answers are not sent. Please avoid including sensitive information or other people’s personal details in free-text answers.

Who handles the information?

Submitted emails and answers are stored in the project’s private Brevo account, accessible to Fintale’s owner. They are not published on GitHub. Brevo processes submissions and sends emails; its services can also record email delivery, opens and link clicks. See Brevo’s privacy policy for its processing and international data-transfer information.

The demo is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub receives technical request information, such as IP addresses, when serving the website. Fonts load from Google Fonts, which also receives the technical information needed to deliver those files. See GitHub’s privacy statement and Google Fonts privacy information.

Temporary drafts and retention

During signup, the demo keeps a temporary copy of your email and answers in this tab’s session storage so you can retry if submission fails. The draft is cleared on the signup success page or when you close the tab. It is not put in the page URL or lesson progress.

Launch-list information is kept while we prepare and communicate the full app’s launch, unless you request deletion sooner. Feedback is used during development. Unsubscribing stops launch emails; to remove your contact record and associated answers, email the address above. A repeat submission using the same email may update that contact’s feedback.

Your choices

You can play without signing up, skip any feedback question, unsubscribe at any time, and ask to see, correct or delete information you submitted. Depending on your location, you may also have rights to object, restrict processing, receive a copy of your data or complain to your local data protection authority. We may need enough information to locate your submission and verify the request.
