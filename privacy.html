YOUR INFORMATION

Fintale privacy notice

Updated 28 October 2026

You can play the demo without sharing your email or feedback. Signing up for launch updates is optional.

Who runs Fintale?

Fintale is an independent project by Marko Simovic. For questions about your information, or to request access, correction or deletion, email simovicm26@gmail.com.

Lesson progress on your device

The demo stores your current lesson step, completed activities and XP in your browser’s local storage so you can return to your place. This lesson progress is not sent to Fintale. Use Restart lesson or clear this website’s browser data to remove it.

Optional feedback

When you choose Send feedback, we save your optional answers about what you liked, what could improve, and which finance, economics and accounting topics you want next. No email or account is required. Feedback is stored separately from the launch list in our Supabase database in Ireland. Each submission includes a randomly generated request ID and a submission time. Feedback helps us develop Fintale; please don’t include sensitive information or other people’s personal details.

Supabase processes technical request information when receiving a submission. Feedback is private to the project owner; website visitors can add responses but cannot read, edit or delete saved responses. See Supabase’s privacy policy for its service processing and international data-transfer information.

Optional email signup

If you choose to subscribe, Brevo receives your email and launch-update consent. We use these to confirm signup and notify you when the full Fintale app is ready, with free access for early signups. Your email is not included in the feedback database. You can give feedback without subscribing, or subscribe without giving feedback.

Consent is given by checking the launch-update box. You can withdraw it through the unsubscribe link in our emails. After a successful signup, the website opens the optional feedback questions; you can skip them. Skipping feedback does not undo signup.

Who handles signup information?

Emails are stored in Fintale’s private Brevo account, accessible to the project owner. Brevo sends confirmation and launch emails and can record delivery, opens and link clicks. See Brevo’s privacy policy. Feedback and email addresses are not published on GitHub.

The demo is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub receives technical request information, such as IP addresses, when serving the website. Fonts load from Google Fonts, which also receives the technical information needed to deliver those files. See GitHub’s privacy statement and Google Fonts privacy information.

Temporary drafts and retention

Your answers, current feedback question and random request ID are kept in this tab’s session storage to support reloads and retries. During email submission, a separate temporary email draft supports retry using your browser’s Back button. The email draft is removed after successful signup; feedback drafts are cleared when you close the tab or restart the lesson. We do not put email addresses or answers in page URLs or lesson progress.

Launch-list information is kept while we prepare and communicate the full app’s launch, unless you request deletion sooner. Feedback is used during development. Unsubscribing stops launch emails; to delete your contact record or a feedback response, email the address above. For feedback submitted without contact information, include enough of your response and its approximate date to help locate it. Older demo versions saved optional feedback in Brevo alongside an email; those earlier records may remain there until deleted.

Your choices

You can play without signing up, skip any feedback question, unsubscribe at any time, and ask to see, correct or delete information you submitted. Depending on your location, you may also have rights to object, restrict processing, receive a copy of your data or complain to your local data protection authority. We may need enough information to locate your submission and verify the request.
